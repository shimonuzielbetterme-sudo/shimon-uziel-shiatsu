# Shimon Uziel Shiatsu

Build a professional personal website for a Shiatsu therapist named Shimon Uziel.

First Visit Experience (very important):
When someone visits the site for the first time, the entire screen shows a large, perfectly centered classic Yin-Yang symbol.
The Yin-Yang circle must take up exactly 80% of the screen (both width and height, maintaining perfect proportions).
The background behind the symbol is pure black.

The symbol slowly and elegantly opens exactly along the curved dividing line between the black and white halves.
The black and white parts separate smoothly in opposite directions, gradually revealing the website underneath.
The animation should feel calm, premium and intentional (duration around 2.5–3.5 seconds) with smooth easing.
Once fully opened, the Yin-Yang fades out gracefully and the full website appears.

Website Style & Content:

Clean, modern, calm and professional wellness aesthetic

Color palette: black, white, soft grays, with subtle warm beige or muted teal accents

High-quality, readable typography

Structure:

Sticky header with the name “Shimon Uziel” and simple navigation (About, Shiatsu, Benefits, Testimonials, Contact)

Hero section with a strong headline, short supporting text, and a clear call-to-action button

Clear sections: – About Shimon – What is Shiatsu – Who it’s for / Benefits – Testimonials – Contact

Key text to include:

Name: Shimon Uziel

Tagline / main message: “Shiatsu therapist who comes to Shiatsu days and restores balance to life”

Phone number: 052-5522602 (make it clickable)

Emphasize that he brings balance back to people’s lives through Shiatsu

The overall feeling of the website should be peaceful, professional, balancing and trustworthy — exactly like a high-quality Shiatsu treatment.

Make the site fully responsive (mobile + desktop) with subtle elegant animations when scrolling.
